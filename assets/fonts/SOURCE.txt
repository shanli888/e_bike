The font files in this directory (SEGClassic.ttf, SEGClassicThin.ttf,
SEGMini.ttf) were extracted from the "speedometer-redesign" design prototype,
where they were embedded as base64 WOFF2 under the @font-face names
SEGClassic / SEGClassicThin / SEGMini. They were converted to TTF with
fontTools so Flutter can load them as bundled assets. The glyph outlines are
unchanged.

DSEG is free for both commercial and non-commercial use, and may be modified
and redistributed under the SIL OFL 1.1. When redistributing the font files
themselves, retain this notice and the OFL 1.1 license text. Bundling the
fonts inside an application does not require the license text to be shipped
(OFL-FAQ 1.10 / 1.11), but this notice is kept for attribution.

Note: the reserved font name is "DSEG".
